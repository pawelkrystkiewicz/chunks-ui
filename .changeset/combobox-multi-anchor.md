Fixed: a multi-select `Combobox` popup anchors to the whole `Combobox.Control`, like single-select, and `Combobox.Chips` spaces its chips. The popup sat at the input's left edge, at the input's width, and closer to the Control's border than the single-select popup sits to its input, because `Combobox.Control` was a plain `<div>` and the popup anchored to the input inside its padding. `Combobox.Control` is now Base UI's `Combobox.InputGroup`: the popup takes the Control's left edge and width and opens `sideOffset` (8px) below its border. The Control renders `role="group"` and state attributes such as `data-popup-open`, `data-disabled`, `data-popup-side`, and a press on its padding focuses the input and opens the popup. `Combobox.Control` must sit inside `Combobox.Root`, and throws outside one. `ComboboxControlProps` are now the InputGroup's props, so `className` may also be a function of the group state. `Combobox.Chips` now has `flex flex-wrap items-center gap-1`, merged with your `className`; the chips sat flush before. An Input placed inside `Combobox.Chips` (Base UI's anatomy) now sits on the chips' row instead of its own line.
