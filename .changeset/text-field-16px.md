Fixed: on iOS, Safari zoomed the page when `Input`, `Textarea`, `NumberField.Input` or `Combobox.Input` took focus, because their text was 14px and iOS zooms into a field with text under 16px. Their text is now 16px below the `md` breakpoint (768px by default, `max-md:text-base`) and 14px from `md` up, as before; `InputCopy`, which renders an `Input`, and your own elements through `inputVariants()` follow. Below `md` a `Textarea` row is 4px taller (24px). A field that takes its width from its content is about 22px wider, because an input's default width follows its font size. These are an `Input` with adornments (`startAdornment`, `endAdornment`, `clearable` or `onClear`), every `InputCopy` and a `NumberField` in a flex row. Give the field a width to keep the old one; on an `Input` with adornments and in `InputCopy`, a string `className` with the width goes on the wrapper. A text-size class you pass, such as `text-lg`, still applies from `md` up. A `sm:` size applies from 640px and wins over the 16px until `md`: with `text-base sm:text-sm`, the field is 14px there and the zoom is back. Unprefixed and `md:`-and-up sizes leave the phone size alone. To change the size below `md`, pass a `max-md:` class, such as `max-md:text-lg`; a size under 16px there brings the zoom back. On an `Input` with adornments and in `InputCopy`, a string `className` goes on the wrapper, so a text-size class there does not change the field: pass a function, `className={() => "max-md:text-lg"}`, which reaches the input. Held sideways, the larger iPhones (such as the Plus, Pro Max and Air models) are wider than `md`, and so is any iPhone with a notch or Dynamic Island on a page that sets `viewport-fit=cover`; there the field is 14px and Safari zooms again. `pointer-coarse:text-base` (Tailwind 4.1 or newer) keeps 16px on touch screens at any width, and the 16px assumes the default root font size, because `text-base` is 1rem.
