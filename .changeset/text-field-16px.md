Fixed: on iOS, Safari zoomed the page when `Input`, `Textarea`, `NumberField.Input` or `Combobox.Input` took focus, because their text was 14px and iOS zooms into a field with text under 16px. Their text is now 16px below the `md` breakpoint (768px by default, `max-md:text-base`) and 14px from `md` up, as before; `InputCopy`, which renders an `Input`, and your own elements through `inputVariants()` follow. Below `md` a `Textarea` row is 4px taller (24px). A field that takes its width from its content is about 22px wider, because an input's default width follows its font size. These are an `Input` with adornments (`startAdornment`, `endAdornment`, `clearable` or `onClear`), every `InputCopy` and a `NumberField` in a flex row. Give the field a width to keep the old one. A text-size class you pass, such as `text-lg`, still applies from `md` up. A `sm:` size applies from 640px and wins over the 16px until `md`: with `text-base sm:text-sm`, the field is 14px there and the zoom is back. To change the size below `md`, pass a `max-md:` class, such as `max-md:text-lg`; a size under 16px there brings the zoom back. On an `Input` with adornments and in `InputCopy`, a string `className` goes on the wrapper: use it for the width, but a text-size class there does not change the field. Pass a function, `className={() => "max-md:text-lg"}`, to reach the input. See Text Size in the `Input` docs.
