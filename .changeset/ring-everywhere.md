`Checkbox.Root`, `Radio.Root`, `Switch.Root`, `Tabs.Tab`, `Tabs.Panel`, `ToggleGroup.Item`, the `Calendar` day and month buttons, `Input` (and your own elements through `inputVariants()`), `Textarea`, `Select.Trigger`, `Combobox.Input`, `Combobox.Control` and `NumberField.Group` now draw the same offset focus ring as Button: 2px of `--ring`, 2px outside the edge, on keyboard focus. Before, their ring sat flush with the edge, with no offset. Each part keeps its transition list, so the ring still shows at once. To keep the flush ring, pass `focus-visible:outline-offset-0` in the part's `className`; it replaces the offset. `Combobox.Control` and `NumberField.Group` draw their ring while the input inside has focus, so there it is `has-[:focus-visible]:outline-offset-0`. On an `Input` with adornments a string `className` goes on the wrapper, so pass a function, `className={() => "focus-visible:outline-offset-0"}`, which reaches the input. `Radio.Item` puts `className` on its label; to keep the flush ring there, build the item from `Radio.Root` and `Radio.Indicator`. The `Calendar` buttons take no `className` of their own, so they keep the offset ring. `Tabs.Tab` is now `rounded-md`, like the indicator under it, so the ring follows the list's corners; to keep square corners, pass `rounded-none` (tailwind-merge replaces it). `Tabs.Content`, which `Tabs.Contents` clips, keeps its inset ring.

`Field.Root` now has `gap-2` (8px) between its label, control and description instead of `gap-1` (4px): the control's ring reaches 4px past its edge, which at 4px met the text around it. To keep the 4px gap, pass `className="gap-1"` on `Field.Root`.
