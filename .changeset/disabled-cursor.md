Disabled form controls show the not-allowed cursor, and the dimmed look has one spelling

- A disabled `Input`, `Select.Trigger`, `Combobox.Control`, `Combobox.Input`, `Combobox.Trigger`, `NumberField.Group`, `NumberField.Input`, `NumberField.Increment`, `NumberField.Decrement`, `Slider.Thumb`, `DatePicker` trigger, `ToggleGroup.Item`, `ClearButton` and a disabled `Calendar` day show the not-allowed cursor, as `Checkbox`, `Switch` and `Textarea` do. Before, `pointer-events: none` hid every cursor over them, so one disabled `Field.Root` showed a default cursor over its `Input` beside a not-allowed cursor over its `Textarea`. They ignore clicks as before: a disabled native input or button takes none, Base UI drops them on `Slider.Thumb`, and the `Calendar` day has no click handler. `Button`, `IconButton`, `Tabs`, the `Accordion` and `Collapsible` triggers, the `Menu` items, `Select.Item` and `Combobox.Item` keep `pointer-events: none` when disabled. To keep the old behaviour on a part that changed, pass `pointer-events-none` in `className`: `disabled:pointer-events-none` on the native inputs and buttons (`Input`, `Select.Trigger`, `Combobox.Input`, `Combobox.Trigger`, `NumberField.Input`, `NumberField.Increment`, `NumberField.Decrement`, `ToggleGroup.Item`, `ClearButton`), `data-disabled:pointer-events-none` on `Slider.Thumb`, `has-[:disabled]:pointer-events-none` on `Combobox.Control`, `has-[input:disabled]:pointer-events-none` on `NumberField.Group`, `[&_button:disabled]:pointer-events-none` on `DatePicker` (its `className` goes on the wrapper) and `[&_td_[aria-disabled]]:pointer-events-none` on `Calendar` (a day has no class of its own).
- The hover styles of `ToggleGroup.Item`, `ClearButton`, `NumberField.Increment`, `NumberField.Decrement`, `Combobox.Clear` and `Combobox.ChipRemove` reset to the rest colour while disabled (`disabled:hover:`, and `data-disabled:hover:` on `Combobox.ChipRemove`, which stays focusable when disabled), because a disabled control now receives the pointer. `ClearButton` no longer scales on a press while disabled. A `hover:` override in `className` still replaces the library's hover style and applies only while the control is enabled.
- The dimmed look of `Radio.Root`, `Radio.Group`, `Checkbox.Root`, the `Menu` items, `Slider.Control`, `Slider.Thumb`, `Combobox.Item`, `NumberField.Increment` and `NumberField.Decrement` is spelled `data-disabled:`, as on `Label` and `Field.Label` (it was `data-[disabled]:`, which tailwind-merge treats as another class, so an override could not replace it). An override in `className` is now spelled `data-disabled:opacity-75`. The `Menu` items keep `pointer-events: none` when disabled.
