# Privacy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

This page covers ui-kit.chunk-creations.com, the Chunks UI documentation site. The `chunks-ui` npm package collects nothing: it has no telemetry and makes no network requests of its own.

## Short version

- No cookies, no ads, no accounts.
- We count page views with Umami, which runs on our own server. It doesn't store your IP address and can't tell who you are.
- Vercel hosts the site and logs requests.
- Your colour mode and the themes you build stay in your browser.

## Page analytics (Umami)

We run our own Umami instance on a server in Germany (Hetzner Online GmbH). When you open a page, the Umami script sends us:

- the page address and title, and the page you came from;
- your browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language;
- your country, region and city, worked out from your IP address.

Umami sets no cookies and doesn't store your IP address. To count visits, it hashes your IP address and browser details together with a salt that changes every month. The hash can't be reversed, and it can't follow you across months or onto other websites. We look only at totals.

We use this to see which pages people read and where they get stuck. The legal basis is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep the data for 24 months.

To opt out, block the analytics script with any content blocker, or run `localStorage.setItem("umami.disabled", "1")` in the browser console on this site.

## Hosting (Vercel)

Vercel Inc. hosts the site. To serve pages and block abuse, it processes request data: your IP address, browser, the address you requested, and the time. Vercel acts as our processor. Vercel keeps these logs only as long as it needs them to run and secure the service, as its [privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy) describes. The data may be transferred to the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a working, secure site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Your browser's storage

The site keeps your light or dark mode choice in your browser's local storage. `/create` also saves your theme and the Accessibility panel's filters there, and so does picking a theme preset on the home page. All of this survives a reload and never leaves your device. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

## Do Not Track

We don't respond to Do Not Track signals. To stop analytics, use the opt-out above.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or erase your data, or to restrict how we use it. You can also object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Analytics data can't be linked to a person, so we usually can't find records that belong to you (Art. 11 GDPR). If you send details that identify them, we will act on them. You can also complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office ([Prezes UODO](https://uodo.gov.pl)).
